Moving isn't just about workouts, fitness or results. It's a way to feel better, find new energy and discover what your body can do.

You don't have to be an athlete or have any experience. There are so many ways to move — from running and dancing to climbing, cycling and entirely new challenges.

Find something that sparks your curiosity. You don't have to be good at it. Just try.
