CHILD & ADOLESCENT PSYCHOTHERAPY

Lumos

In person · online parent consultations · online for older adolescents

Lumos is a child and adolescent psychotherapy practice through which Nikolina Đurić provides psychotherapeutic support to children and adolescents and works with their parents. Her aim is to create a safe space in which a child or young person can be heard, understand themselves better and develop ways of coping with the challenges they face.

FIRST CONTACT

An introduction to the reason for getting in touch, an exchange of basic information and discussion of next steps.

FOR WHOM

Children, adolescents and their parents.

FORMAT

In person and online, depending on the type of support and age.

PROFESSIONAL

Nikolina Đurić, child and adolescent integrative psychotherapist, social worker and owner of Lumos.
