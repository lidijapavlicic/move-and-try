COOKIE POLICY

29 October 2026

Technologies we use

MOVE&TRY uses cookies, browser local storage (localStorage) and temporary session storage (sessionStorage). Not all of these are HTTP cookies.

Essential and functional storage

moveandtry_cookie_consent_v1 remembers analytics consent; moveandtry_language remembers language; moveandtry_vote_* indicates a previous vote; temporary storage may help Surprise Me avoid repeating an activity.

Duration depends on the storage type and browser settings.

Optional analytics

With consent, Google Analytics 4 may set _ga and _ga_* cookies to measure visits. Analytics must not load before consent. Cookie lifetimes are distinct from Google Analytics data-retention settings.

Managing consent

On your first visit, you can accept or reject optional analytics or review settings. Change your choice later using Privacy settings. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics loading and the website attempts to remove accessible analytics cookies. You can also clear browser data.

Third parties

Supabase, Formspree and infrastructure providers may process technical information to provide their services. See our Privacy Policy.
