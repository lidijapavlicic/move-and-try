TRY · CREATE

YOUR FIRST DRAWING OR PAINTING.

Start with an object in front of you. See what you can do with a pencil or a little colour.

EXPLORE THE GUIDE ↓

START WITH SOMETHING IN FRONT OF YOU.

Choose a cup, a pear or a small branch. Look at how tall it is compared with its width and notice where the light comes from. Sketch its overall shape lightly, then add the details.

TRY THE SAME SUBJECT IN COLOUR.

Choose two or three colours and see what tones you can mix. Paint an area in the light and an area in shadow. See what changes when you soften the edge of the shadow.

IF YOU WOULD LIKE SOME GUIDANCE.

When you contact a studio, tell them what you want to try and whether you have any experience. If you like the idea of drawing and painting, ask which to start with and what to bring to your first class.
