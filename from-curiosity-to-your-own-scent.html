From Curiosity to Your Own Scent: A New Activity on MOVE & TRY

Sometimes a new activity on MOVE & TRY starts very simply — with an invitation to try something.

This time, the invitation came from Biljarica, to join a natural perfume-making workshop.

It was one of those experiences where you don't just learn something new, but spend a few hours completely immersed in a different world — scents, notes, combinations and the process of creating something that eventually becomes entirely your own.

That experience became a new activity on MOVE & TRY — Natural Perfume Making.

From experience to a new activity

We published a guide for anyone who wants to understand what a first encounter with natural perfume making looks like.

Alongside the guide, we also published a real experience from the workshop — what it feels like to choose fragrance notes, build your first combinations, change what doesn't quite work and finally smell a perfume you created yourself.

But this time, the story didn't end with simply discovering a new activity.

We also launched Where to Try

Alongside the new activity, we launched a part of the platform we had been planning for a long time — Where to Try.

The idea is simple: discovering something interesting isn't always enough. We want to help curiosity take one more step and become a real experience.

So now, alongside an activity, you can also find a place where you can actually try it.

Biljarica is the first place featured for natural perfume making, and we'll continue expanding Where to Try as new activities and real opportunities to experience them are added to MOVE & TRY.

Discover here. Try there.
