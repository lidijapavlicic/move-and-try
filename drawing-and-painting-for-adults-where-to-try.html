REGULAR COURSES FOR ADULTS

BAKART STUDIO

KNEZA BORNE 14 · ZAGREB

At Bakart Studio you work at your own pace in a small group, with individual guidance from academically trained painter Natasha Bakovic. You can explore the basics of drawing, proportion and perspective, work with composition, colour, light and shadow, and try acrylic or oil painting.

WHO IT IS FOR

Adult beginners and people with previous experience

FORMAT

Small groups with individual guidance

SCHEDULE

Tuesday 10:00–12:30
Wednesday 11:00–13:30
Thursday 17:30–20:00

MATERIALS

Starter materials are included in the price, except brushes.

PRICES

  • One drawing or painting session (2.5 hours) · €40
  • Drawing course (6 sessions) · €220
  • Painting for new students (4 sessions) · €130 promotional price
