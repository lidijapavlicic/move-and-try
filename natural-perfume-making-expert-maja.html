NATURAL PERFUME IS MORE THAN JUST A SCENT.

On natural perfumery, the personal experience of scent and why creating your first perfume does not require any previous knowledge.

EXPERT INSIGHT

Maja Gašparić

Biljarica · natural perfumery and perfume-making workshops

Making a natural perfume is not only about creating a scent. It is also a process of getting to know yourself, your own experience of fragrance and your own sense of smell.

Participants do not need any previous knowledge or experience before the workshop. By smelling, exploring and combining different notes, they gradually discover what they like and create a perfume that is completely their own.

There is no single “right” perfume.

It is especially important to me that people understand there is no single “right” perfume. Scent is a very personal experience, and that is exactly the beauty of natural perfumery: through different plants, essential oils and other natural aromatic materials, everyone can discover a fragrance story of their own.

That is why the workshop is at once a little exploration, a creative process and a lot of enjoyment in scent.

“You do not need to know anything about perfume to create your own. Curiosity and a willingness to explore scents are enough.”
