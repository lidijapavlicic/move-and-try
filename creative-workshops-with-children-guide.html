CREATIVE WORKSHOPS WITH CHILDREN

You don't need to know how to draw, paint or make anything special.
Just sit down together and start.

EXPLORE THE GUIDE ↓

01 / WHAT TO EXPECT

YOU GET AN IDEA. THEN YOU MAKE IT YOUR OWN.

At a creative workshop, you'll usually find the materials, a few simple instructions and an idea to get you started.

You can follow the example, make something completely different or simply see where the colours, paper, clay, shells or a completely new material take you.

There is no single “right” result. That's part of the fun.

02 / DO YOU NEED TO PREPARE?

USUALLY — NO.

Unless the organiser says otherwise, the materials and everything you need will usually be waiting for you at the workshop.

GOOD IDEA

Wear something that won't be a disaster if it gets a little messy.

EVEN BETTER

Arrive without expecting everything to turn out perfectly.

03 / WHY TRY IT TOGETHER?

BECAUSE THE MOST IMPORTANT THING IS HAVING FUN TOGETHER.

A creative workshop is first of all a chance to spend time with your child in a slightly different way.

No rush, no need for anything to be perfect, and plenty of space to choose, imagine, experiment and laugh together at whatever appears on the table.

And while a child draws, glues, cuts, shapes, chooses colours and comes up with their own solutions, they're also developing creativity and imagination, fine motor skills and hand–eye coordination along the way.

The best result may not be the thing you take home.
It may be the time you spent together.

04 / FIRST STEP

CHOOSE SOMETHING THAT SOUNDS FUN.

You don't need to find the perfect workshop or know in advance whether your child will love it.

Choose a theme that catches your attention, arrive without big expectations and make something of your own.

If there is a little paint on your hands, something new on the table and a good moment you shared together — the try worked.

05 / THE VERDICT

HOW DID IT GO?
