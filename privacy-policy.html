PRIVACY POLICY

29 October 2026

Who operates MOVE&TRY?

MOVE&TRY is an independent platform developed by Lidija Pavličić as an individual in Croatia. For privacy questions contact hello@moveandtry.com.

Information we process

Activity suggestions: suggestion text, record ID and submission timestamp stored in Supabase; name and email are not required.

Votes: activity slug, reaction, record ID and timestamp in Supabase, plus a local browser indicator of a previous vote.

Submitted experiences: name, email, activity and responses voluntarily provided through Formspree. Submission does not automatically authorize public publication.

Messages: email address and message content.

Push subscriptions: where enabled and requested, endpoint, encryption-related subscription values, technical identifiers or hashes, and timestamps.

Technical data and analytics: infrastructure providers may process information needed to serve and secure the website. With consent, Google Analytics 4 may process visit, browser, device and approximate location data.

Purposes and legal bases

We process information to operate and secure the website, respond to requests, receive suggestions and experiences, record votes and deliver requested notifications. Depending on the activity, the legal basis may be legitimate interests, steps taken at your request or consent. Analytics requires consent before activation.

Service providers and transfers

We use Supabase for interactive data, Formspree for submissions, Google Analytics 4 for consent-based analytics, and hosting/network providers. Some processing may take place outside the EEA; actual locations and safeguards depend on the provider and configuration.

Retention

Google Analytics event-data retention is set to 2 months; other analytics data and aggregated reports may have different retention. Suggestions, votes, submissions, messages and push subscriptions are retained only as needed for their purpose or applicable legal requirements. Contact us to request deletion.

Your rights

Depending on applicable conditions you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact hello@moveandtry.com. You may complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

Updates

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change.
