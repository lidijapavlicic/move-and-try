CREATIVE WORKSHOPS · MOSAIC MAKING

LUCE SUNCE

GAJNICE 20 · ZAGREB, CROATIA

Luce Sunce creative centre organises mosaic-making workshops led by Miroslav Bušić. It's an opportunity to discover the materials, tools and basic steps involved in creating your own mosaic. No previous experience is needed.

WHO CAN JOIN

Adults; according to the organiser, school-age children may also participate.

INSTRUCTOR

Miroslav Bušić

EXPERIENCE

None required.

MATERIALS

Provided, according to the organiser's listing.

DATES

Check with the organiser for the next workshop cycle.

LOCATION

Luce Sunce creative centre, Gajnice 20, Zagreb

ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

From your first design to a finished piece.

At the workshop, you'll discover the basics of mosaic making, get familiar with tiles and tools, choose a simple design and begin arranging your own mosaic.

According to Miroslav, one of the most rewarding moments for beginners is grouting, when the individual pieces begin to look like a complete work.

For information about duration, price, available places and the next workshop cycle, contact the organiser directly.
