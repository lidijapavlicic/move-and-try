TRY #001 · MOVE

RUNNING

You don't need to become a runner.
Let's just see if you like running.

HOW IT STARTED

I started running when I was a teenager.

I'd quit all the sports I was doing — mostly out of teenage rebellion — but I still wanted to do something. I just didn't really want to admit it, or have anyone know I was doing it.

So I went running.

I was already fit, so I ran about five kilometres from the start. I didn't have a running watch. I didn't have a training plan. I didn't know anything about pace, heart-rate zones or running technique.

This was before social media made recreational running something you saw everywhere. As far as I knew, people who ran were marathon runners.

I wasn't a marathon runner. I just went running.

And then I started testing my own limits. If I had more time, I'd see if I could go farther. If I had more energy, I'd see if I could go faster.

I didn't know how to be a runner.
I just tried running.
